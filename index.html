Google Photos Locked Folder — Complete Guide

The Google Photos Locked Folder is a built-in privacy feature designed to hide sensitive images and videos from your main photo grid. When an item is moved to the locked folder, it is protected by your device's screen lock (PIN, pattern, or biometric) and becomes invisible to other apps on your phone.

Key Fact

By default, items in the Locked Folder are not backed up to the cloud. If you lose your phone or uninstall the Google Photos app without turning on Locked Folder backup, those photos are permanently deleted.

Locked Folder vs Hidden Album

Many users confuse the two. A standard "hidden album" merely moves photos out of the main timeline. The Google Photos Locked Folder goes further: it encrypts the files on the device level (or cloud if backup is enabled) and requires authentication to view. Third-party apps cannot scan or access files within the Locked Folder.

How to Set Up Google Photos Locked Folder

Setting up the feature for the first time is straightforward. Ensure your Google Photos app is updated before beginning.

1

Open Utilities

Open the Google Photos app. Tap Library at the bottom right, then tap Utilities.

2

Find Locked Folder

Scroll down to the 'Organize your library' section and tap Locked Folder.

3

Authenticate

Tap 'Set up Locked Folder'. You will be prompted to verify your device's screen lock (fingerprint, face, or PIN).

4

Move Photos

Select photos from your main grid, tap the three-dot menu, and select Move to Locked Folder.

Can I access Locked Folder Google Photos on PC?

Yes, as of recently, Google allows web access. Go to photos.google.com, click on 'Locked Folder' in the left sidebar, and enter your Google account password. (Note: Cloud backup for the locked folder must be enabled on your phone first).

Troubleshooting Guide

If you are locked out or experiencing issues, here is how to recover access safely as the device owner.

Issue Safe Owner Recovery Steps
Forgot your device PIN? The Locked Folder uses your phone's lock screen PIN. If forgotten, you must perform a full device reset through your Google or Apple account recovery. Warning: Resetting the phone deletes local Locked Folder data if backup wasn't on.
Change Google Photos locked folder password You cannot change it independently. You must go to your phone's main Settings > Security and change your device screen lock PIN/Pattern. The Locked Folder will automatically use the new one.
Photos disappeared after new phone transfer Items in the Locked Folder do not transfer automatically via USB or transfer apps. You must enable 'Backup Locked Folder' on the old phone, then sign in on the new phone.
